How to Build a Website With AI in 2026

A beginner-friendly step-by-step guide to planning, creating, customizing and publishing a website using today's AI website builders.

Building a website used to mean choosing a template, learning a website editor and manually creating every page. AI website builders can now handle much of the initial setup for you.

You still need to make decisions about your business, audience and content, but AI can help turn those decisions into a working starting point much faster.

What Is an AI Website Builder?

An AI website builder uses artificial intelligence to assist with parts of the website creation process. Depending on the platform, this can include generating layouts, suggesting pages, producing draft text, creating images and helping configure basic website features.

The important thing to remember is that AI-generated websites should still be reviewed and customized. Your website needs accurate information, your own branding and content that genuinely represents your business.

How to Build Your Website With AI

STEP 1

Decide What Your Website Needs to Do

Before choosing an AI builder, define the main purpose of your website.

  • Promote a local or small business
  • Generate enquiries or leads
  • Sell products online
  • Showcase a professional portfolio
  • Publish articles or educational content
  • Create a landing page for a service

Knowing the purpose first makes it much easier to choose the right website builder and avoid paying for features you don't need.

STEP 3

Tell the AI About Your Website

Most AI website builders begin by asking questions about your business or project.

Give the AI clear information about what you do, who the website is for, the services you provide and the style you want.

The better your instructions are, the more useful the first version of the website is likely to be.

STEP 4

Review the AI-Generated Website

Don't publish the first version immediately.

Check the page structure, navigation, text, images, contact information and calls to action. Remove generic content and replace it with details specific to your business.

STEP 5

Customize Your Branding

Add your own logo, brand colors, photographs and business information. A website should look like your business rather than a generic AI template.

Pay particular attention to the homepage because it often forms a visitor's first impression of the business.

STEP 6

Check the Mobile Version

Always review your website on a phone before publishing.

Make sure text is readable, buttons are easy to tap, images fit the screen and important information is easy to find.

STEP 7

Prepare Basic SEO

Before launching, give each important page a clear title and description. Use headings that accurately explain the content and make sure pages link naturally to other useful pages on your website.

SEO isn't about repeating keywords everywhere. The priority should be creating pages that answer what visitors are actually searching for.

STEP 8

Connect Your Domain and Publish

Once the website has been reviewed, connect your domain and publish it. After launch, test the website again on both desktop and mobile.

Check every navigation link, contact button and form to make sure everything works correctly.

Can a Beginner Really Build a Website With AI?

Yes. AI website builders can reduce many of the technical barriers that previously made website creation difficult for beginners.

However, AI doesn't remove the need for human judgment. You still need to check the information, improve the content, select appropriate images and make sure the finished website accurately represents your business.

Think of AI as a website creation assistant rather than a replacement for making those decisions yourself.

Final Checklist Before Publishing

  • Business name and contact information are correct
  • Navigation links work
  • Website displays properly on mobile
  • Images are relevant and properly sized
  • AI-generated text has been reviewed
  • Page titles and descriptions are completed
  • Contact forms and buttons have been tested
  • Domain is connected correctly
